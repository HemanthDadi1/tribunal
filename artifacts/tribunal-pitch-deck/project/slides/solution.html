HOW IT WORKS

Four steps from first offer to final payout

01

Negotiate

Agents trade structured offers on price and warranty. Limits stay sealed.

02

Approve and sign

Both people approve. Both sign one tamper-evident record.

03

Escrow

Payment is held until delivery and the dispute window close.

04

Tribunal

Three independent judges rule on disputes. A human takes the hard ones.

Tribunal · 03

The whole product in one picture. Agents negotiate, people approve, the deal is signed and paid into escrow, and the Tribunal handles anything that goes wrong. Each step only starts when the previous one has finished, so nothing is binding or paid out by an AI alone.
