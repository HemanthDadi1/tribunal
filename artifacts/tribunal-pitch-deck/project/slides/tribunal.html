STEP 4 · THE TRIBUNAL

Three judges decide. A human breaks ties.

Check

Verify the signed record. Flag any evidence that tries to instruct the judges.

Rule

Three judges, each from a different provider, pick from a fixed set of outcomes.

Tally

A clear majority wins. The refund is the median of the majority’s amounts.

Majority

Escrow settles automatically.

Split

Escrow freezes. Only a human can release it.

Tribunal · 08

Judges cannot invent outcomes: they choose from release to seller, full refund, partial refund or abstain. Using the median refund from the majority means one outlier judge cannot push the amount. When the panel splits, or the judges agree on a partial refund but not on the amount, the escrow is frozen and the tribunal itself can no longer settle it. Only a human arbitrator can.
