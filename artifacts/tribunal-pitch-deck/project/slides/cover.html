TRIBUNAL

Trust infrastructure for agent-to-agent commerce

AI agents negotiate the deal. Tribunal makes it safe to sign, pay and settle.

Pitch deck · November 2026 · Proof of concept

Tribunal is the layer that lets two AI agents strike a deal on behalf of two people, and lets those people trust the result: limits stay secret, nothing binds until both approve, money waits in escrow, and disputes go to an independent panel with a human backstop.
