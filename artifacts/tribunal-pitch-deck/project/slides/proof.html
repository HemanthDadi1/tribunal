Tribunal · 06 · Source: demo.py stress test (seed 1); 1,364 closed by agents, 245 by mediator

Markets were generated at random: values between 10,000 and 60,000 rupees, random limits, random toughness. Of 2,000 markets, 1,611 had a deal that worked for both sides, and Tribunal closed 1,609 of them, 245 through the mediator. Leakage was measured in long negotiations: a plain concession curve offered within 100 rupees of the seller’s true floor in 652 of 652 cases; with the stop-short margin, 0 of 882. Joint surplus per market rose from 4,802 to 5,192 rupees when warranty was negotiable.
