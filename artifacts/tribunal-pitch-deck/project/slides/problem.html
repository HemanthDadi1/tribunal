THE PROBLEM

Agents can already haggle. Nobody can trust the deals they make.

They leak

A plain agent concedes step by step until it reaches your limit, so the other side learns exactly what you’d pay.

They overreach

A language model can be talked into accepting a deal its owner never authorised.

Nobody settles

When the item arrives broken, there is no record, no escrow and no referee.

Tribunal · 02

Agent commerce is coming whether or not it is safe. Today an agent that negotiates for you has three failure modes: it reveals your limit by conceding to it, it can be manipulated into a bad deal, and when something goes wrong there is no shared record and no one to decide. Tribunal fixes all three.
