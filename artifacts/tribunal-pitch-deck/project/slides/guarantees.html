STEP 1 · WHY IT’S SAFE

Six rules the negotiation can’t break

Strategy is sealed

The language layer never sees private limits, so it can’t leak them.

Random stop-short

Agents stop a private, random margin before their limit.

Hard mandate guard

A separate check blocks any offer or acceptance outside the owner’s mandate.

Price and warranty

Trading the two lets both sides come out ahead.

Strict messages

Every offer is JSON checked against a whitelist. Nothing free-form gets through.

Blind mediator

If talks stall, it splits the overlap at a random point, revealing neither limit.

Tribunal · 05

These six rules are enforced in code, not left to a model’s judgement. The most important one is the separation: the part that decides numbers is deterministic and holds the limits; the part that talks never sees them. That is what makes it safe to put a language model on top later.
