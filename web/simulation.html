      Market settings (changes restart the run)

      Assumptions (illustrative figures, not measured data)
      • 120 buyers' agents, 40 laptop sellers and 40 hotels. Honest sellers have a problem on 3% of deals; misrepresenting sellers on 35%, and are 30% more likely to be picked today because they list lower prices.
      • Laptops cost ₹15,000–60,000 and arrive in 1–3 days. Hotel stays cost ₹2,000–9,000 a night for 1–3 nights, with check-in 4–48 hours after booking.
      • Laptop problems: dead on arrival (full refund) or battery below promise (12% refund). Hotel problems: no room (full refund) or no pool (25% refund).
      • Today, buyers pursue 35% of problems under ₹5,000 and 70% above. A claim takes 7–45 days and costs ₹600 to handle. Buyers win 75% with good evidence, 40% with poor, and 30% of false claims succeed.
      • With Tribunal, agents file 97% of problems. Each judge is right 92% of the time with good evidence and 72% with poor, and abstains 30% of the time on poor evidence. Splits and appeals go to a human (95% right, 2–5 days).
      • Reputation updates when a ruling lands. Buyer agents avoid sellers who lose often; sellers losing over 20% of 20+ deals are suspended.
